Welcome to Our Community

Sage is Southern Methodist University's premier LGBTQ+ social organization, creating a vibrant, inclusive space for all students to connect, celebrate, and thrive.

Who We Are

We're a dynamic student organization dedicated to fostering community, promoting awareness, and creating memorable experiences for LGBTQ+ students and allies at SMU.

What We Do

  • Regular social events
  • Community Outreach and Advocacy
  • Leader Development Opportunities
  • Safe Space for Connection and Support

Join Us

Whether you're looking to make new friends, find support, or get involved in campus activism, there's a place for you here. Our meetings are welcoming, casual, and open to all SMU students.

Ready to join our community? Come to our next meeting or event - no formal membership required! All are welcome.
